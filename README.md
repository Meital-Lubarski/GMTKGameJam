# 👻 When She Goes Silent

A first-person 3D survival horror game created for **GMTK Game Jam 2026**.

You spend the night inside an abandoned school where a little girl mysteriously disappeared. Soon, you discover that the rumors were true — a ghost is hunting you through the empty halls.

But there is a catch:

**The closer she gets, the quieter she becomes.**

Listen carefully, manage your flashlight, keep moving, and survive for as long as you can.

🎮 **[Play When She Goes Silent on itch.io](https://snir3308.itch.io/when-she-goes-silent)**

## 👻 Gameplay

The goal is simple: **survive for as long as possible.**

Explore the dark school while a ghost continuously hunts you. Instead of relying only on what you can see, you must also listen to your surroundings — the ghost becomes quieter as she approaches and louder when she is farther away.

### Main Mechanics

- 🔦 **Flashlight system** — use your flashlight to defend yourself against the ghost
- 👻 **Ghost pursuit** — the ghost continuously searches for and chases the player
- 🔋 **Limited battery** — flashlight energy is a limited resource that must be managed carefully
- ⚡ **Battery pickups** — explore the environment to restore flashlight power
- 🔊 **Proximity-based audio** — the ghost's sound changes depending on her distance from the player
- 🏃 **Survival gameplay** — keep moving, avoid the ghost, and survive for as long as possible
- 📈 **Increasing difficulty** — the threat becomes more dangerous as the game continues

## 🔦 The Flashlight

The flashlight is your main defense against the ghost.

Using its stronger light can temporarily stop the ghost, but it consumes significantly more battery. This creates a constant tradeoff between protecting yourself now and conserving enough power to survive later.

## 🎧 Sound as a Game Mechanic

Sound is not just atmosphere — it is part of the gameplay.

The ghost behaves in an intentionally unusual way:

> **The quieter she becomes, the closer she is.**

Players must pay attention to the ghost's audio while navigating the school, creating tension between what they can see and what they can hear.
